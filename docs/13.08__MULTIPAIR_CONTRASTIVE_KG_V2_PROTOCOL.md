# Multi-pair contrastive KG verifier v2

## 1. Research question

For components predicted as a clinically high-risk anatomy, does a fold-safe,
dual-hypothesis anatomical relation score improve exact class-swap warnings
beyond alternative-class probability and pair-identity controls?

This experiment tests the KG as the primary contribution. Visual evidence is
not part of v2 and remains a future unary evidence family.

## 2. Frozen cohort and target

- Source: 3,837 components from five OOF validation cases.
- Test split: prohibited.
- Include components whose predicted-class clinical weight is 3.
- Create one hypothesis row for each exported anatomical alternative class
  `1–30` other than the predicted class. Background (`0`) has no anatomical
  relation profile and is excluded rather than assigned a fabricated KG node.
- Expected cohort: 723 components, 1,412 anatomy candidate hypotheses. Of these,
  689 components have two candidates and 34 have one after Background exclusion.
- Positive candidate: `CLASS_SWAP`, dominant GT fraction at least 0.5, and the
  candidate alternative equals the dominant GT class.
- Primary warning level: component. Candidate risks are aggregated by maximum,
  preserving the corresponding alternative as the explanation hypothesis.
- Candidate-level and supported-pair metrics are secondary.

GT supplies evaluation targets only. It is never an inference feature.

## 3. Fold-safe KG

For each held outer fold, relation profiles come only from
`outer_fold_<fold>_train/anatomy_pair_relations.csv`. For a predicted hypothesis
and an alternative hypothesis, a partner anatomy is eligible only when both
hypotheses have:

- at least 20 pooled relation observations;
- at least three station-conditioned training rows.

Station is marginalized as a latent mixture weighted by train-fold relation
observations. Filename station, held-case GT station, and held-case GT anatomy
are prohibited from the deployable methods.

For every visible eligible predicted partner, compute distance/touch
compatibility under both hypotheses. The KG contrast is:

```text
KG-LLR = compatibility(alternative hypothesis)
         - compatibility(predicted hypothesis)
```

Missing partners are `UNKNOWN` and contribute zero. Tool/entropy reliability is
an audit stratum, not a binary gate. No inpainting or tool removal is used.

## 4. Frozen ablations

| ID | Inputs | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | alternative probability raw | probability baseline |
| M1 | probability + predicted/alternative identity | mandatory pair shortcut control |
| M2 | KG contrast + pair identity | KG-only separability |
| **M3** | **probability + pair identity + KG contrast** | primary KG gain |
| M4 | probability + pair identity + shuffled-partner KG | identity negative control |
| M5 | probability + pair identity + anatomy-presence vector | non-relational context control |
| M6 | confidence + probability + pair identity | uncertainty control |

All learned methods use class-balanced L2 logistic regression. Scaling and
training use the other four cases only in each leave-one-case-out split.

## 5. Primary gate

M3 must beat both M0 and M1 at component level with all of:

1. macro-case AUPRC delta at least +0.02;
2. at least 4/5 held cases improved;
3. descriptive case-bootstrap lower bound above zero;
4. non-tiny macro-case AUPRC decrease no worse than −0.01;
5. minimum-case PPV decrease no worse than −0.05 at 5% and 10% coverage;
6. M4 and M5 do not reproduce the complete gate.

Report component/candidate AUPRC, AUROC, Brier, pair-macro AUPRC for ordered
pairs with at least five positives across at least two cases, warning PPV/recall
at 5/10/20/30%, directions, area,
tool/reliability, and evidence availability.

## 6. Safety interpretation

This experiment is exploratory because the five validation cases and danger
taxonomy have no clinician adjudication and pair selection derives from the OOF
corpus. A positive result cannot authorize auto-correction or ontology loss.
A negative result does not invalidate anatomical knowledge; it rejects this
specific train-GT relation representation for the current warning endpoint.
