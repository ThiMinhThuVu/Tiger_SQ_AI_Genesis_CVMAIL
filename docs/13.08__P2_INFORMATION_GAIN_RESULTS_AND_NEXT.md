# P2 information-gain results and next experiment

## 1. Decision

P2-DENSE remains the locked verifier baseline. Neither the frozen DINOv2 ROI
branch nor the pre-specified gated AA-018 branch demonstrated incremental
information beyond P2 under the frozen gate.

The joint visual-plus-knowledge model must not be run: V4 and K3 both failed
their independent entry gates.

K4's interval versus raw P2 was `[-0.032366, +0.084797]`; its probability of a
positive mean case delta was 0.7991. This is insufficient with only five cases
and 3/5 improvements.

## 3. What went well

1. The experiment tested genuinely new inputs. Visual features came from RGB +
   OOF predicted masks; AA-018 came from fold-train relation statistics and
   OOF spatial predictions. Neither branch was another transform of P2.
2. Leakage controls held: 70 OOF frames, five validation cases, no test data,
   and GT used only for the endpoint or fold-train knowledge statistics.
3. V2 exactly reproduced P2-DENSE ranking at `0.373769`, proving the visual
   comparison did not benefit from a weakened matched baseline.
4. Missing class-6 partner anatomy was treated as UNKNOWN, never as a
   violation. Station information was isolated to non-deployable K5.
5. Negative controls were informative. Shuffled ROI (`0.347580`) harmed P2 less
   than real ROI (`0.277386`), and even filename-station oracle did not help.

## 4. What failed and why it matters

### 4.1 Current visual representation is not usable evidence

- ROI-only AUROC was `0.4398`, below random ordering.
- V4 decreased AUPRC in every case, in predicted-7 and predicted-19 directions,
  and in both tiny and non-tiny components.
- `344/744` components had less than 0.25 DINO patch-token support and required
  bbox fallback. Nevertheless, adequate-support components also degraded:
  this is not only a tiny-mask problem.
- The encoder is generic frozen DINOv2 and the representation pools tokens from
  a full-frame resize. It likely captures case/view appearance more strongly
  than the subtle morphology distinguishing fatty tissue from lymph node.

Therefore more PCA dimensions, probability transforms, or fusion weights must
not be tuned on these five cases. That would optimize noise rather than test a
new evidence source.

### 4.2 AA-018 is meaningful anatomy but weak discrimination for CF005

AA-018 states that fatty tissue around the esophagus should be attached to or
near the esophagus. It does not establish that lymph nodes cannot also be near
the esophagus. The relation is therefore compatible with anatomy but not
necessarily discriminative between classes 7 and 19.

The binary reliability gate was highly case-dependent: gate-on ranged from
18.9% in case 11 to 78.9% in case 10. This created a case-level shortcut and
removed useful observations. K3 consequently degraded high-precision warning
behavior: minimum-case PPV changed by −0.10 at 5% coverage and −0.125 at 10%.

### 4.3 K4 cannot yet be called knowledge gain

K4 is the only method above raw P2 on mean AUPRC, but it failed stability and
semantic audits:

- only 3/5 cases improved;
- the case-bootstrap interval crossed zero;
- non-tiny components worsened substantially while tiny components improved;
- learned signs contradicted the intended ontology in all five folds for some
  redundant features: the class-7 distance-violation coefficient and class-19
  compatibility coefficient were negative in 5/5 folds;
- strong multicollinearity exists between distance, envelope violation, touch,
  and direction interactions.

Thus K4 may contain generic spatial/context signal, but the present gain cannot
be attributed to AA-018 reasoning. It is a hypothesis generator, not a passing
knowledge verifier.

## 5. Next primary experiment: dual-hypothesis relational likelihood ratio

The next knowledge test should ask a discriminative question:

> Given the observed component location and the other visible anatomies in this
> frame, is its relational pattern more compatible with class 7 or class 19?

For each outer fold, use only the four training cases to build two relation
profiles from GT:

```text
H7  = component is Fatty tissue esophagus
H19 = component is Lymph node
```

For every observable partner anatomy, estimate under H7 and H19:

- boundary-distance envelope;
- touch probability;
- number of observations and independent cases;
- an uncertainty/shrinkage weight.

At inference, use the OOF predicted component and predicted partner masks to
compute a relation log-likelihood ratio:

```text
R-LLR = log P(observed relations | counterpart hypothesis)
        - log P(observed relations | predicted-class hypothesis)
```

Missing partners remain UNKNOWN and add zero evidence. Partners are retained
only when both hypotheses have sufficient fold-train case support. This makes
the score comparative and pair-discriminative, unlike a one-sided AA-018 rule.

## 6. Next visual experiment, secondary priority

If compute permits, replace full-frame token pooling with a representation that
actually resolves the object:

1. fixed square crop centered on the predicted component with pre-specified
   context padding;
2. resize crop to the encoder input resolution;
3. extract a task-aligned OOF segmentation-encoder embedding;
4. build fold-train class-7 and class-19 visual prototypes;
5. use counterpart-versus-predicted prototype margin as the new visual score.

This is preferable to another PCA/logistic variation because the feature has a
clear hypothesis meaning: which anatomy prototype does the observed ROI
resemble? DINO crop can remain a control, not the primary representation.

## 7. Safety and publication interpretation

- Keep P2-DENSE as the only current baseline worthy of independent validation.
- Do not run the joint model, auto-correction, or ontology loss.
- Do not present K4 as evidence that the ontology works.
- Frame the negative V4/K3 results as a disciplined ablation: naive feature
  fusion and one-sided anatomical rules do not beat a strong probability
  baseline.
- A publishable positive claim now requires either R3 or the visual prototype
  margin to pass on newly frozen experiments and later on independent cases.

partner identities, which argues against an anatomy-knowledge interpretation.

### Updated next decision

Do not add more station/relation variants on these same five cases. The last
defensible automated information-gain experiment is a high-resolution,
component-centered visual crop with fold-train class prototypes and an explicit
counterpart-versus-predicted prototype margin. If that fails, the project needs
new independent cases and clinical review rather than more feature engineering.
